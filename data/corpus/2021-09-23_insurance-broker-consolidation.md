# Decision Memo: Insurance Broker Consolidation

**Prepared by:** Dana Whitcombe, VP Finance
**Reviewed by:** Risk Committee (R. Okoro, P. Lindgren, T. Bauer)
**Decision date:** 2021-09-23

## Background

Since 2016 Northwind has carried three separate commercial insurance brokers: Harrow Mutual Brokerage (property & cargo), Cedarline Agency (auto/fleet liability), and Beck & Forsythe (workers' comp and general liability). This grew organically through two acquisitions and was never rationalized. The result is duplicated renewal cycles, three sets of certificate-of-insurance requests, and inconsistent loss-run reporting. Finance spends roughly 90 staff-hours per quarter reconciling premium allocations across the three.

## What we evaluated

We ran an RFP in Q2 2021 with five brokers. Total annual brokerage commissions across the three incumbents were ~$418K on ~$6.1M of premium. The fragmentation was not a coverage-resilience problem — coverage has been adequate and we have had no material gaps. This is purely a back-office efficiency and negotiating-leverage question.

Finalists were Meridian Risk Partners and our incumbent Beck & Forsythe (who bid to absorb all lines).

## Decision

Consolidate all commercial lines onto **Meridian Risk Partners**, effective at the 2022-01-01 renewal. Meridian wins on (a) a single unified renewal calendar, (b) a dedicated account team with a freight/logistics book, and (c) a fee-based model ($310K flat advisory fee) replacing commission, which improves transparency and is projected to save ~$108K annually versus blended commissions.

## Tradeoffs we accepted

- **Single-broker concentration.** A broker is an intermediary, not an underwriter — our actual risk transfer stays spread across multiple carriers, so we judged concentration risk here as low. If Meridian underperforms, switching brokers is a 60–90 day exercise, not an operational outage.
- **Transition friction.** Loss-run history migration and re-issuing ~340 certificates to customers will take Q4 2021. Cedarline's mid-term auto policy runs to March 2022; we will let it lapse naturally rather than pay cancellation short-rate penalties.

## Action items

1. Notify Harrow, Cedarline, Beck & Forsythe of non-renewal (Whitcombe, by 2021-10-01).
2. Stand up Meridian onboarding; transfer loss runs (Okoro, Q4 2021).
3. Re-paper customer COI templates (Procurement, by 2021-12-15).

Reviewed and approved by the Risk Committee, 2021-09-23.
