# Decision Memo: Temporary Hiring Freeze — Operations

**From:** Dana Okafor, VP Operations
**To:** Ops department heads, Finance (cc: P. Reyes, CFO)
**Decision owner:** Dana Okafor

## Decision
Effective 2023-09-01, Operations will pause all external backfill and net-new hiring through 2023-12-31. This is a temporary freeze, not a reduction in force. We are targeting **$1.4M in unspent H2 payroll** against a board-level commitment to trim Operations run-rate by 6% before year-end.

## Context
Q2 actuals came in 4.8% over our operating plan, driven mostly by overtime in the Memphis and Reno cross-dock yards and by three dock-supervisor reqs we opened in May. Freight volumes are soft — tender acceptance is down roughly 9% year-over-year — so we are carrying more loaded labor capacity than the network needs right now. Rather than touch existing headcount, we can hit most of the gap by simply not adding to it.

## What is frozen
- All open and pending external reqs in Ops (currently 11 open, ~$1.1M annualized).
- Backfills for voluntary attrition, with two carve-outs below.

## Carve-outs (require my written approval)
1. **Safety-critical roles** — yard safety leads, DOT compliance. We do not gamble on these.
2. **Single points of failure** — if one departure leaves a yard or shift uncovered, we backfill. Tanya in Reno is the example everyone raised; that req stays live.

## Tradeoffs we discussed
We know freezing reqs pushes more overtime onto current crews, which partially eats the savings. Finance modeled this: even assuming OT claws back ~25% of the gross, we net roughly $980K. We also accept some onboarding lag in Q1 2024 when the freeze lifts — recruiting will pre-stage candidate pipelines so we are not starting cold.

## Review
Revisit at the November ops review. If tender volumes recover above plan for two consecutive months, we lift early. This memo supersedes the July guidance that left backfills to manager discretion.

— Dana
