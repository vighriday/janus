Devon,

Thanks for the redline. We're close, but two items still don't work for us.

**1. On-time SLA threshold.** Your draft sets the on-time delivery SLA at 92% measured monthly, with penalties only below 88%. Our 2023 actuals with Westmark are sitting at 90.4% YTD, so an 88% penalty floor means we'd basically never collect on missed service. We need the penalty tier to start at 91%, graduated: 2% credit on the lane's monthly spend at 89–91%, 5% below 89%. That's the same structure we run with our other regional carriers — not a one-off ask.

**2. Detention.** You want free time held at 2 hours, then $65/hr. We're proposing 2 hours free, $55/hr, capped at $400/load. The cap matters more to us than the rate; last winter we ate four loads at the Dayton DC where detention ran past 9 hours each because of *our* dock, and uncapped that's brutal for both sides.

To be clear — this is purely about the renewal terms. We are NOT moving volume off Westmark or changing the routing setup; you're still our primary on the Midwest reefer lanes and that's not on the table. This is just getting the contract language to match how the lanes actually perform.

Marcus, can you confirm Finance is fine with the $400 cap exposure? Hannah modeled it at roughly $9–11K/yr worst case across the network.

If we land 91% trigger + $400 cap, I think we sign this week.
