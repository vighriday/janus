# Decision Memo: Midland Hub Lease Renewal

**Prepared by:** Renata Okafor, Director of Facilities & Real Estate
**Reviewed by:** Tom Delacroix (VP Operations), Priya Venkataraman (Controller)
**Decision:** APPROVED — execute 7-year renewal with Cedar Hollow Property Group.

## Background

Our current lease on Building C at the Midland regional hub (138,000 sq ft cross-dock plus 11,000 sq ft office) expires 2024-02-28. We have occupied this site since 2016. The landlord, Cedar Hollow Property Group, opened renewal talks in May proposing a jump from our current **$6.40/sq ft/yr** (NNN) to **$8.95/sq ft/yr** — a 40% increase they justified on regional warehouse comps.

## What We Negotiated

After three rounds with their broker (Hallmark Commercial), we landed at:

- **$7.55/sq ft/yr** for years 1–3, **$7.95** years 4–5, **$8.30** years 6–7 (fixed escalators, no CPI clause).
- Landlord-funded **$420K** in dock-door and LED relighting improvements (we had quoted $610K to do ourselves).
- **5 months** abated rent up front, applied against the buildout overlap.
- Expanded trailer yard easement — **22 additional drop slots** at no incremental ground rent.
- Early-termination option at month 60 with 9 months' notice and a $185K fee.

## The Tradeoff

Relocating to the Pinewood Industrial Park alternative (quoted $6.90/sq ft) would have cost an estimated **$2.3M** in moving, racking re-permitting, and ~6 weeks of throughput disruption during peak. Staying avoids that entirely and locks the yard expansion we need for the Q3 volume forecast (+14% YoY). Blended effective rate over the term lands near **$7.10/sq ft** after abatement and TI credit — below Cedar Hollow's opening ask and only marginally above today.

## Decision & Next Steps

Priya signed off on the NPV (favorable vs. relocation by ~$1.6M over 7 years). Legal to finalize estoppel and SNDA by 2023-10-13. Renata to coordinate the relighting schedule so it does not collide with the November peak freeze. Facilities will revisit the month-60 termination option in mid-2027.
