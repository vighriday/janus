# Q4 Peak-Season Temp Staffing Review

**Reviewers:** Marisol Tran (Ops Planning), Dev Okafor (Hub Operations), Priya Nandakumar (Finance liaison)
**Scope:** Riverton and Castlebrook cross-dock hubs, Oct 30 – Dec 10, 2023.

## What we set out to check
Whether our temp-labor ramp for peak matched actual throughput. Last year we got burned by understaffing the **peak dispatch window** (the 4 AM–9 AM block where outbound trailers must be sealed before the regional line-haul cutoff). This year we deliberately overcorrected.

## What the numbers say
We brought on 142 temps across both hubs via Brightline Staffing and Cedar Grove Labor (split roughly 60/40). Budgeted spend was $710K; actuals landed at $824K — 16% over, driven mostly by overtime we authorized in week 2 before throughput data was in.

The uncomfortable finding: at Castlebrook, utilization during the peak dispatch window averaged 71%. We had warm bodies standing at staging lanes with no trailers to load because the inbound side hadn't been scaled to match. We staffed the dock for a volume the yard couldn't feed.

Riverton was tighter — 88% utilization — because Dev's team staggered start times in 30-minute waves instead of a single 4 AM call. That alone cut idle hours by an estimated 340 hours over the period.

## Decision
For 2024 peak we are: (1) capping initial temp headcount at 110 and holding a 25-person on-call tier we can activate within 48 hours via Brightline; (2) adopting Riverton's staggered-wave start model at both hubs; (3) requiring inbound-yard scaling to be approved jointly with dock staffing, not separately. Priya will hold OT authorization until day-5 throughput is confirmed.

We are NOT changing staffing vendors — Brightline's fill rate (94%) was fine. This was a planning-sequencing problem, not a sourcing one.
